# Prompt Engineering — Vanderbilt University

## What is this?

A course focused on prompt engineering and effective interaction with generative AI systems.

## Why did I learn this?

I wanted to understand how to use AI tools more effectively for learning, research, problem solving, and technical work instead of treating AI as a simple answer generator.

## What I learned

- Principles of effective prompting
- Structuring instructions and context
- Improving the quality and reliability of AI outputs
- Iterative prompting and refinement
- Practical use of AI for different tasks

## How will this help me?

I plan to use these skills as a supporting tool in my academic and engineering work, while keeping the actual technical understanding and implementation my own.
